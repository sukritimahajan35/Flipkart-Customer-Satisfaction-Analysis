# Customer Satisfaction Towards Flipkart – Market Research & Statistical Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project examines customer satisfaction and loyalty towards Flipkart using primary survey data collected through a structured Google Forms questionnaire.

The study follows a quantitative research approach and analyses customer perceptions across different dimensions such as product quality, delivery reliability, pricing, website and app usability, payment security, return processes, and customer support.

The analysis also examines the relationship between customer satisfaction and customer loyalty using statistical techniques and data visualisation.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of the study are:

- To study the overall level of customer satisfaction towards Flipkart.
- To identify key factors influencing customer satisfaction.
- To analyse customer perceptions of Flipkart's website and mobile application.
- To examine satisfaction with delivery, return, and refund policies.
- To understand the relationship between customer satisfaction and customer loyalty.

---

## 📊 Dataset

The study uses **primary data** collected through a structured questionnaire using Google Forms.

The questionnaire contains four major sections:

1. Personal & Demographic Details
2. Online Shopping Behaviour
3. Customer Satisfaction
4. Customer Loyalty

The Customer Satisfaction section uses a **5-point Likert scale**, ranging from:

- 1 – Strongly Disagree
- 2 – Disagree
- 3 – Neutral
- 4 – Agree
- 5 – Strongly Agree

The final dataset contains **108 valid responses**.

---

## 🔬 Research Methodology

The study follows a **descriptive research design** and uses a quantitative approach.

### Sampling

- Sampling Technique: Non-Probability Convenience Sampling
- Target Population: Flipkart users
- Data Collection Method: Online questionnaire
- Sample Size: 108 respondents

The questionnaire was distributed through digital platforms and respondents were required to have prior experience shopping on Flipkart.

---

## 📌 Key Findings

### Customer Satisfaction

The mean customer satisfaction score was **4.380 out of 5**, indicating a high overall level of satisfaction among respondents.

### Customer Loyalty

The mean customer loyalty score was **4.182 out of 5**, indicating strong loyalty and positive behavioural intentions towards Flipkart.

### Satisfaction–Loyalty Relationship

Pearson correlation analysis showed a positive relationship between customer satisfaction and customer loyalty:

**r = 0.587, p < 0.001**

This indicates that higher customer satisfaction is associated with stronger customer loyalty.

### Customer Behaviour

- 50.9% of respondents were aged 18–25.
- 57.4% reported shopping on Flipkart 2–3 times per month.
- 63.9% had been using Flipkart for more than 5 years.
- Students represented the largest occupational group at 41.7%.
